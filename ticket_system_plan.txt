Important SLA Rules
1. Working Days
All SLA calculations are based on working days (WD), not calendar days.
2. Optional Parts Order
Parts Order is required only when the necessary parts are not available.
If all required parts are already available, the ticket can move directly from Approval → Work Start.
3. Parts Arrival
Parts Arrival applies only to tickets where a Parts Order has been created.
4. Work Start
Work should start within the specified SLA after approval, provided the required parts are available.
5. Resurvey
Resurvey is applicable only when required by the Insurance Company/Surveyor.
6. Final Closure
The ticket is considered fully completed only after the vehicle is delivered to the customer and the final SLA is marked complete.
